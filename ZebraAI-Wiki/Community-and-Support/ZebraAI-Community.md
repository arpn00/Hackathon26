# What is the ZebraAI Community? 
The ZebraAI community is a growing contingent of CSS Engineers and others who use AI to innovate and experiment safely and compliantly with case data. With more than 17k users, 12k experiments, and almost 4 million prompts (as of December 2025), our users are making an impact in their work and on their broader teams. 

# How do I connect with the ZebraAI Community? 
The best way to connect with us is through our [ZebraAI Team](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/team/19%3A1Pvl3cRXmwYrC0nv1jFV7NZj6qQLGbgv0EeGvg6swqc1%40thread.tacv2/conversations?groupId=af998ff3-0fad-4654-b368-5249e20ada9e&tenantId=72f988bf-86f1-41af-91ab-2d7cd011db47) channels.
