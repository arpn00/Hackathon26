# What are ZebraAI Ambassadors? 
Ambassadors are the power users of ZebraAI, and they are committed to the ZebraAI community. Our ambassadors are at the forefront of experimentation within the ZebraAI, pushing into new territory and collaborating with our development team. They are also active in our community, answering questions and helping mentor new users.
