# ZebraAI Reporting
ZebraAI Reporting provides visibility into platform adoption, model performance, experiment activity, and community engagement. Use these reports to monitor usage trends, compare models and experiments, understand user participation, and identify opportunities to improve the ZebraAI experience.

### [Model Performance & Usage](https://msit.powerbi.com/groups/fb5b92e7-8d59-46e7-b2ed-2d75ca1b1e97/reports/708690a3-b501-42c2-a20b-758e39e1a6c8/73aec58919507128e0a5?experience=power-bi)

Provides a model-level comparison of performance and consumption across ZebraAI. The report separates API and interactive prompt activity and shows median response time, token consumption, usage rate, and average daily inferences by model. Use it to identify the fastest, most consistently used, and most resource-intensive models and to inform model selection and capacity decisions.

### [Shared Experiment Report](https://msit.powerbi.com/groups/fb5b92e7-8d59-46e7-b2ed-2d75ca1b1e97/reports/708690a3-b501-42c2-a20b-758e39e1a6c8/51db16c34527e7f0552c?experience=power-bi)

Compares experiments that have been shared with the broader ZebraAI community. It combines community-engagement measures—such as favorites and likes—with API and prompt usage rates and average daily activity. Use it to discover popular reusable experiments, understand whether engagement translates into sustained use, and see whether an experiment is primarily consumed through the API or prompt interface. Sort columns to see various metrics. e.g., Top experiments by likes, highest usage, etc.

### [User Engagement and Activity](https://msit.powerbi.com/groups/fb5b92e7-8d59-46e7-b2ed-2d75ca1b1e97/reports/708690a3-b501-42c2-a20b-758e39e1a6c8/2694764ac41c257bff33?experience=power-bi)

Provides a people-centered view of ZebraAI adoption and participation. It shows recent user growth and ranks users by experiment ownership, contributions, and combined engagement, with filters for organizational and user attributes. Use it to monitor adoption, identify active experiment creators and collaborators, and understand which parts of the organization are participating in ZebraAI.

### [Experiment Summary](https://msit.powerbi.com/groups/fb5b92e7-8d59-46e7-b2ed-2d75ca1b1e97/reports/708690a3-b501-42c2-a20b-758e39e1a6c8/16e11340213ee2d0741e?experience=power-bi)

Provides an aggregate view of the overall ZebraAI experiment portfolio. It summarizes experiments by active status, experiment action or type, sharing level, target audience, and engagement measures such as favorites, likes, and feedback. Use it to understand the composition and health of the experiment estate, rather than the performance of one specific experiment.

### [Site Stats](https://msit.powerbi.com/groups/fb5b92e7-8d59-46e7-b2ed-2d75ca1b1e97/reports/708690a3-b501-42c2-a20b-758e39e1a6c8/7e3fa9aa5a00ea04c1c1?experience=power-bi)

Provides the detailed, experiment-level drill-down for ZebraAI. Its searchable table brings together each experiment’s title, owner, active status, sharing level, type, experiment runs, unique users, API calls, and additional engagement measures. Use it to investigate individual experiments, compare their adoption and activity, identify owners, and find experiments with especially high or low usage. Reported totals should be treated as filtered, point-in-time values rather than permanent figures.
