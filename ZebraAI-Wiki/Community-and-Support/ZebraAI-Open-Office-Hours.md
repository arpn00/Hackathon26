#XIO Citizen Development Office Hours

ZebraAI Office Hours are expanding! Bring your questions, blockers, and in-progress ideas to this open community session. We’ll cover topics across the AI citizen development space, with rotating experts available to work with you directly.

## Programs & Technologies in scope:

*   ZebraAI / Supportability Hub
*   Extensibility (Agency, API, Copilot Hub, MCP, VSC Extension)
*   OAP (Copilot Studio and AI Foundry)
*   Prompting / Context Engineering
*   Innovation Centralization Program (ICP) and Citizen Development
*   Test Kitchen (Agents, Skills, DfM)

## What to expect:
❓ Open Q&A
<br>🚧 Help with blockers
<br>💡 Guidance on in-progress ideas
<br>👥 Practical support from rotating experts
