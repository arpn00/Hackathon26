Sharing your app with other users[](https://cnfe.visualstudio.com/ZebraAI/_wiki/wikis/ZebraAI/284/How-to-build-and-deploy-ZebraAI-Power-Apps-connector-in-your-own-environment?anchor=6.-sharing-your-app-with-other-users)

After you have build and deployed your ZebraAI Power Apps connector in your own environment, you have the ability to share it with others.<br>
To share your app with other users, we've added a role to make getting/giving access easier.

### Experiment Owner:

*   Go to "My Experiments," edit API, and add “role_dataversecaller."

### User who isn't an owner:

*   Go to [CoreIdentity](https://coreidentity.microsoft.com/manage/Entitlement/entitlement/zebraai-lmrc)  and request "DataverseCaller"
