# How to Check ZebraAI Core Identity Level Access and Expiration in CoreIdentity
## Overview
This wiki documents how to check in CoreIdentity for the role(s) a user has in ZebraAI. Additional information on ZebraAI roles and requesting access can be found at [Get Started with ZebraAI - Overview](https://cnfe.visualstudio.com/ZebraAI/_wiki/wikis/ZebraAI/93/Get-Started-with-ZebraAI). 
## Steps to Check ZebraAI Access and Expiration in CoreIdentity
1. Go to [CoreIdentity](https://coreidentity.microsoft.com/). 
2. Select "Entitlements" from the left menu
3. Select "My Memberships" tab
4. Look for "ZebraAI" under "Entitlement Name" and look across to "Role." 
The role listed there is your role for ZebraAI. You can also see the expiration date for your entitlement in the next column.
