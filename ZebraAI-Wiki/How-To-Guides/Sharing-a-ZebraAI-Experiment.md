# Sharing a ZebraAI Experiment
## Overview
This wiki documents how to share a ZebraAI experiment. 

## Prerequisites
### 1. Contributor-level access to ZebraAI Core 
If you don’t have access yet or only have reader access, request contributor-level access via [CoreIdentity](https://coreidentity.microsoft.com/) and ensure you're connected via MSFT-AzVPN-Manual. 

### ❓Not sure if you have contributor-level access? 
Here's how to check: [How to Check ZebraAI Access in CoreIdentity](/How-To-Guides/Platform-Basics/How-to-Check-ZebraAI-Access-in-CoreIdentity)

## Steps to Sharing a ZebraAI Experiment
1. From your experiment, click "Edit Settings" in the top right. 

| ![Screenshot 2025-08-26 161826.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202025-08-26%20161826-ba5d3425-1978-4fb9-ae32-e7723f8de961.png) |
|--|

2. Under the "Experiment" tab, you can change the Share Level using the drop down. 

| ![Screenshot 2025-08-26 161858.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202025-08-26%20161858-2d27acc1-4a1b-4620-adf5-40d9adf3519c.png) |
|--|


3. Change the Share Level from "Private" to "Shared.

- _If you only see "Private" in the Share Level list AND you have verified that you have contributor-level access [How to Check ZebraAI Access in CoreIdentity](/How-To-Guides/Platform-Basics/How-to-Check-ZebraAI-Access-in-CoreIdentity), here are some steps for troubleshooting: [Troubleshoot Issues with ZebraAI Contributor-Level Access - Overview](https://cnfe.visualstudio.com/ZebraAI/_wiki/wikis/ZebraAI/217/Troubleshoot-Issues-with-ZebraAI-Contributor-Level-Access)_

4. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click "Update" to save your changes. 

| ![Screenshot 2025-08-26 162018.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202025-08-26%20162018-a129d736-5ed2-4e20-a297-dc00851650dc.png) |
|--|

5. If you want to send someone a link to your experiment, you can use the "Share" button in that same area. 

| ![Screenshot 2025-08-26 162053.png](/.attachments/Screenshot%202025-08-26%20162053-bb665bd7-dde9-4b34-99cf-6ab2845ba66c.png) |
