# AI Chat

Use this AI Chat experience when you wish to interact with a ChatGPT in a safe and secure environment.

**Note**: You can also use AI Chat to review your ZebraAI experiment prompts for potential errors, including PII handling, template structure, and output evaluation.

![Chat](/wiki/imgs/Chat.png)

This chat experience comes with a number of useful controls, Model Selection, Temperature adjustment,  Top P adjustment, Source (code) selector, Send, Reset, and Chat to Clipboard.

##Model Selection

Model Selection allows you to choose from a variety of GPT models as shown below.  Each model has advantages and disadvantages.  GPT 4 tends to be more accurate than GPT 3.5 for example, but GPT 4 models except for GPT-4o can be quite slow.

![Models](/wiki/imgs/Model-Select.png)

##Temperature

In AI, Temperature is a parameter that controls the randomness of the output generated by models like GPT. It influences how creative or predictable the responses will be:  

**High Temperature (>1)**: Increases randomness, leading to more diverse and creative outputs, but with a higher chance of producing less coherent or nonsensical text.  

**Low Temperature (<1)**: Decreases randomness, making the output more predictable and coherent, but potentially less varied and creative. 

Use the slider to increase or decrease Temperature.

##Top P

Top P (also known as nucleus sampling) is a parameter that controls the diversity of the generated text by considering only the most probable tokens. Here’s how it works:

**Top P = 0.1**: The model considers only the top 10% of the most likely next words. This makes the output more predictable and less diverse.

**Top P = 0.9**: The model considers the top 90% of the most likely next words. This increases the diversity of the output, allowing for more creative and varied responses.

By adjusting the Top P value, you can fine-tune the balance between creativity and coherence in the AI’s responses. Use the slider to increase or decrease Top P.

## Send

The Send button submits your prompt to the selected model.

## Reset

The Reset button starts a new session, resetting all controls to default settings.
