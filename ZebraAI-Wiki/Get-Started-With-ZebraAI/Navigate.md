# Navigating ZebraAI

The ZebraAI Home page is your launch pad into the world of ZebraAI.  All functions are available on the collapsable menu to the left of the page, see the left side of the picture below.  By selecting a menu option, you can explore a variety of tools and support options.  The Home page also provides some helpful information and videos for those starting their AI journey with ZebraAI.  The latest ZebraAI news is also available on the Home page.

![Home](/wiki/imgs/Zebra-home.png)

Let's break down what's available on this page.  

## The Left Side Menu  
  
The menu is the primary means for navigating ZebraAI.  All functions are available through the menu. Here is a brief summary:  
  
<a target="_blank" href="https://zebra-ai-web-prd.ait.microsoft.com/home">ZebraAI Home</a>: Overview of the platform and its features, as seen above.  
[Chat](/Get-Started-With-ZebraAI/Chat): Engage with chatGPT in a secure environment.  
[Explore ZebraAI Experiments](/Get-Started-With-ZebraAI/Explore-ZebraAI-Experiments): Browse and learn from existing AI experiments.  
[Create An Experiment](/Get-Started-With-ZebraAI/Create-An-Experiment): Set up and run your own AI experiments.  
[My Experiments](/Get-Started-With-ZebraAI/Navigate/My-Experiments): Explore and maintain your experiments.  
[Request CSS Data Usage](/How-To-Guides/Request-CSS-Data-Usage): When your experiment is ready you can submit a request to use real CSS case data.  
[Revoke CSS Data Usage](/How-To-Guides/Revoke-CSS-Data-Usage): When your experiment is complete you can remove access to real case data.  
  
[Getting Started with SkillsBuddy](/Integrations/Getting-Started-with-SkillsBuddy): Gen-AI app that simulates realistic customer interactions to help advocates to practice and improve their communication skills through AI-generated scenarios and feedback.  
[TMaaS](/TMaaS/TMaaS): A machine learning solution that aim to infuse advanced data science into daily business operations.  

[Admin](/Internal-ZebraAI-Resources/Admin): A set of tools for managing experiments, parameters and prompts.  
  
[ZebraAI Community](https://cnfe.visualstudio.com/ZebraAI/_wiki/wikis/ZebraAI/291/ZebraAI-Community): A link to the ZebraAI Teams Channel. <br>
<a target="_blank" href="https://zebra-ai-web-prd.ait.microsoft.com/help"> Help/FAQs</a>: Helpful information about ZebraAI.  
<a target="_blank" href="https://zebra-ai-web-prd.ait.microsoft.com/changelog">Change Log</a>: The change history for ZebraAI.  
[Provide Feedback or File Bugs](/Community-and-Support/Provide-Feedback-or-File-Bugs): A form for submitting bugs or otherwise providing feedback to the ZebraAI team.

## The Home Page Body  

The body of the Home page can be broken into three sections.  The topmost section, introduction provides a brief introduction to ZebraAI and a helpful video for those just getting started.  The middle section includes buttons for the four most common activities in ZebraAI:  

![buttons](/wiki/imgs/Home-buttons.png)  
  
- The [**Explore**](/Get-Started-With-ZebraAI/Explore-ZebraAI-Experiments) button allows you to browse available experiments.  
- The [**Create**](/Get-Started-With-ZebraAI/Create-An-Experiment) button opens the experiment creation editor where you can create your won experiments.  
- The [**Try**](/Get-Started-With-ZebraAI/Chat) button opens the chat feature available in ZebraAI.  This is just like ChatGPT but is secure.  
- The <a target="_blank" href="https://zebra-ai-web-prd.ait.microsoft.com/help">**Learn**</a> button opens the Help/FAQs page which is loaded with helpful information to guide you in your use of ZebraAI  
  
Finally, the bottom of the Home page is where the ZebraAI team shares the latest news about ZebraAI and the ZebraAI Community.
