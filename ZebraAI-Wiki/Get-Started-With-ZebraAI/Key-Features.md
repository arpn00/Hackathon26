# ZebraAI Key Capabilities
ZebraAI is a powerful tool to support and enhance your productivity.  ZebraAI supports a wide variety of capabilities, some of which are listed here.

## Intelligence
-	ZebraAI supports **multiple pre-trained GPT Models** including Access to pre-trained models like GPT-4, GPT-4o, and GPT-4o mini. For more information on the benefits of each model, see [Models](/Reference/ZebraAI-Platform/Models).
-	ZebraAI includes easy **sample prompt access** with the ability to share and reuse prompts. For more on shared and sample prompts, see [Explore ZebraAI Experiments](/Get-Started-With-ZebraAI/Explore-ZebraAI-Experiments).   
-	ZebraAI supports **contextual understanding**, conversation context monitoring for relevant responses and follow-up information. ZebraAI can:  
    -	*Understand the conversation's flow*, comprehending and remembering the ongoing dialogue's context, allowing it to generate responses that are coherent and relevant within the conversation's framework. 
    -	*Maintain referents*, correctly interpreting referents like "it," "that," or "this" based on prior conversation details. 
    -	 *Adapts to User Intent*, discerning user intent beyond just literal meanings, considering the tone, implied meanings, and user preferences expressed earlier in the dialogue.
    -	*Provides Follow-Up Information*, offering additional related information or suggestions based on the context of previous interactions.
-	ZebraAI features customization, **control over model output** and ability to set response creativity parameters.

##Data
-	ZebraAI generates **synthetic data** for safe experimentation until approval criteria is met. Using synthetic data has the following benefits:
    -	*Privacy Protection*: Synthetic data doesn't involve real individuals, thus preserving privacy while being used in analysis and AI training. It eliminates the risk of exposing sensitive information.
    -	*Bias Mitigation*: By carefully designing synthetic data, it’s possible to address and reduce inherent biases found in real-world datasets. This helps in training fairer AI models.
    -	*Controlled Scenario Testing*: Synthetic data allows for the creation of specific, controlled scenarios to test various models and systems extensively without the unpredictability of real-world data.
    -	*Data for Rare Events*: It enables the generation of data for rare or edge cases that might not be well represented in existing datasets but are essential for robust model training.
-	ZebraAI supports dynamic **multiple sourced data** insertion into prompts, including support case data.  For more information see [Dataflow](/Reference/Data/Dataflow).
-	Augmentation Generation Hybrid search with **vectorization service** and search endpoint, providing the following benefits:
    -	*Improved Relevance*: By combining both search methods, hybrid search systems can return results that are more semantically relevant and contextually aware.
    -	*Enhanced User Experience*: Users get more accurate and useful results, improving their overall search experience.
    -	*Flexibility*: Hybrid search can handle a wide range of queries, from specific keywords to broader, more abstract concepts.

## Content and Learning
-	ZebraAI provides an interactive environment through a **simple web interface** for entering text prompts and receiving generated responses. Try it at [ZebraAI](https://zebra-ai-web-prd.ait.microsoft.com/home).  
-	ZebraAI continually collects and reports experiment **feedback**. ZebraAI and the ZebraAI team never stop learning. We are committed to constantly improving ZebraAI to better fit your needs and to grow as a more powerful tool. This way we can adapt to your changing needs and ZebraAI can remain a useful tool in your toolbox. 
-	ZebraAI supports iterative test and refinement through LLM response evaluation via **prompt test runs** allowing for continuous prompt refinement. In this way, you can keep learning along with us. Every experiment can be extended in real time through follow-up chat sessions where you can question your experiment results and discover new ways to adapt your experiments to better fit your needs. The ZebraAI team is committed to ensuring that experiment prompts are easily adapted, allowing you to edit and refine your prompts while running your experiments.
-	ZebraAI supports extensibility through API to **extend experiments** inside other applications. This makes any experiment available to your application, providing the simplest way for you to integrate AI into your application. For more about calling an experiment through the ZebraAI API see the [Help and FAQ](https://zebra-ai-web-prd.ait.microsoft.com/help) page.

## SkillsBuddy - AI-powered Training Application
-	**AI-powered training application** that simulates realistic sales and support interactions using large language models to help customer advocates practice and refine their communication skills in a risk-free environment
-	**Dynamic scenario generation** creates diverse customer personas and conversation contexts, enabling advocates to practice through a chat-based interface with realistic, engaging interactions
-	**CARE framework evaluation** provides real-time feedback and coaching based on Communication, Accountability, Resourcefulness, and Empathy to help users build confidence and improve performance
-	**Multilingual support and customizable settings** allow for flexible training experiences that can be tailored to specific organizational needs and repeated for continuous learning
-	**Training and assessment tool** designed for sales practice, support skill development, and helping trainers and managers evaluate advocate performance and identify coaching opportunities
