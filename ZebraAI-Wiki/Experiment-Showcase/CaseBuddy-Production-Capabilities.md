 # ZebraAI Use Cases in CaseBuddy
 
CaseBuddy is emerging as a key front-end for ZebraAI-backed support scenarios. It gives support teams a familiar place to surface AI capabilities against real case workflows, which makes it a strong vehicle for moving useful experiments closer to day-to-day operational use.
 
## Why CaseBuddy
 
CaseBuddy is valuable as a ZebraAI endpoint because it sits close to the actual support motion. Instead of asking engineers to leave their workflow and open a separate experimentation experience, CaseBuddy can bring AI-assisted insights into the same place where case context, decision-making, and follow-through already happen. This reduces friction, improves discoverability, and makes it easier to test which ZebraAI scenarios create real operational value.
 
## Current ZebraAI-backed use cases in CaseBuddy
 
### Similar Case discovery
 
One of the clearest use cases is helping engineers find similar historical cases faster. This includes experiences such as **Similar Case** and **Simple Search for Nostalgic / CSS Visual KB**, where ZebraAI is used to search across past support signals and return relevant cases, patterns, and likely resolutions.
 
This use case helps reduce time spent manually searching across fragmented systems and gives engineers a faster path to proven troubleshooting approaches.
 
### Customer context and “Know Me”
 
**Know Me for CaseBuddy** is focused on giving engineers a more complete picture of the customer by combining past case data and relevant product context into a single experience.
 
This helps teams understand customer history more quickly, reduce context switching, and better tailor support engagement for strategic or high-value customers.

 ### Scoping assistance
 
**Scope Assist (Scoring)** applies AI to the scoping process by evaluating and scoring scoping efforts. The goal is to reduce manual effort, create more consistency in how scoping is assessed, and improve throughput.
 
This is a strong example of ZebraAI being used not just for content generation, but for operational guidance inside a support workflow.

[Scope Assist: User guide](https://internal.evergreen.microsoft.com/en-us/topic/8609d0e8-e2aa-b9bf-3f75-71189be8c4d9)
 
 ### Case transfer analysis
 
**Case Transfer Agent** focuses on one of the most expensive and frustrating support patterns: unnecessary or poorly informed case transfers. ZebraAI is used to analyze transfer conditions and support better transfer decisions.
 
The value of this use case is improved engineer productivity, lower transfer overhead, and a better customer experience when cases move between owners or teams.
 
 ### Language translation
 
**Language Translator - Expanded Language Support** addresses the cost and complexity of non-English support by using ZebraAI to translate support content more efficiently.
 
In CaseBuddy, this kind of capability can help make multilingual support workflows more scalable and consistent, while reducing delays caused by language barriers.

 ### Incident postmortem support
 
 **Incident Postmortem (IPM)** applies automation and AI to speed up the creation of post-incident reviews.
 
This use case is important because post mortems are valuable but often labor-intensive. Bringing ZebraAI into this flow can reduce manual effort and improve the speed and consistency of incident learning.

 ## Common value across these use cases
 
Across these scenarios, the pattern is consistent: ZebraAI in CaseBuddy is most useful when it helps support teams do one of the following:
 
 - find the right case knowledge faster
 - understand customer and case context more quickly
 - reduce repetitive manual analysis
 - improve decision quality in support workflows
 - accelerate actions that are currently slow, fragmented, or difficult to scale
 
In other words, CaseBuddy is not just a place to expose AI outputs. It is becoming a practical surface for applying ZebraAI to high-friction support tasks where speed, consistency, and context matter.

 
 ## Summary
CaseBuddy is a strong fit for ZebraAI because it can surface AI-assisted workflows directly in support operations. The current scenario set centers on similar-case discovery, customer context, scoping support, transfer analysis, language translation, and incident post mortem acceleration. Together, these use cases show how ZebraAI can move from experimentation into practical, workflow-level impact.
