## 📌 Overview

LittleAnt Copilot is a centralized web-based platform that combines multiple Zebra AI experiments into a single experience for case management.

  

It provides:

- AI-generated case summaries 

- Scoping quality insights 

- Aged case analysis 

- Escalation detection and categorization

  

---

  

## 🧠 How It Works

LittleAnt chains multiple Zebra AI experiments together to optimize speed and results, rather than relying on a single model or experiment type.
  

### Core capabilities

- **Case Summarization** – Quick understanding of case status 

- **Scoping Analysis** – Identifies strong vs. weak scoping 

- **Aged Case Review** – Auto-analysis for cases >30 days 

- **Escalation Monitoring** – Detects multiple escalation types 

- **Delay Insights** – Highlights blockers and root causes
