🛂 How to Access ZebraAI

If you're going to create an experiment, you will need to request Contributor access. Otherwise, requet Reader access.

To request ZebraAI access:

  1. Go to ZebraAI Entitlement on Core Identity
  2. Click “Request Membership”
  3. Select your account and the role you'd like to request
  4. Choose “For Myself”
  5. For Business Justification input “ZebraAI access"
  6. Click Submit

Once you receive access, you may need to log out and back in to ZebraAI for changes to take effect.
