##What Is One Agentic Platform (OAP)? 
OAP (One Agentic Platform) is Microsoft’s secure, compliant, enterprise-grade platform for building, governing, deploying, and operating AI agents—supporting both low‑code and pro‑code development, with full lifecycle management, observability, and integration with CSS systems.
