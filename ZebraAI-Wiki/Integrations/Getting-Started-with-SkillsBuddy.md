# What is SkillsBuddy?

SkillsBuddy is a Generative AI app that helps sales and support advocates practice customer interactions in a risk-free, simulated environment. It provides real-time feedback and coaching based on the CARE framework (Communication, Accountability, Resourcefulness, Empathy).

  

# Who is SkillsBuddy for?

SkillsBuddy is designed for:

- New hires ramping up their skills

- Experienced advocates looking to refine specific techniques

- Trainers and managers who want to assess and coach team members

  

# How does SkillsBuddy work?

1. You select parameters like product area, scenario type, difficulty, and language.

2. The AI generates a realistic customer profile and scenario.

3. You chat with the AI as if it were a real customer.

4. After the session, you receive feedback and coaching tips based on your performance.

# How to start a conversation?

- You need to select the Skill, Business line, Difficulty level of customer, Language of communication, and provide Scenario (optional).
- Click on the **‘Start Simulation’** button.
- You need to send the first message (greet and inquire). The response will be generated, and you can continue the conversation, one message at a time.
- Click the **‘End/Get Evaluation’** button to end the conversation and receive evaluation feedback based on the chat.



# Where can I leave feedback about the chat experience?

You can provide feedback on the chat experience by clicking the **‘Send Feedback’** button and selecting the thumbs up/thumbs down button and provide details to submit.



# What is the CARE framework?

CARE stands for:

- **Communication**: clarity, active listening

- **Accountability**: ownership, minimizing customer effort

- **Resourcefulness**: providing correct solutions, adding value

- **Empathy**: connecting positively, personalizing the interaction

  

# What kind of feedback will I get?

You’ll receive:

- Scores for sub-skills (e.g., probing questions, credibility)

- Personalized coaching tips (e.g., “Try asking more open-ended questions”)

  

# Can I use SkillsBuddy in my language?

Yes. SkillsBuddy supports multiple languages via GPT-4o mini and will expand with Azure OpenAI models.

  

# Is my data safe?

Yes. SkillsBuddy uses secure storage and access controls. Only minimal personal data (e.g., alias) is logged, and it's stored securely.

  

# What are the limitations of SkillsBuddy?

- Evaluates one conversation at a time (no memory across sessions)

- Text-only interaction

- Feedback is based on AI interpretation and may not always be perfect

- Designed only for Microsoft-related sales and support scenarios

  

# Can I rely solely on SkillsBuddy for training?

No. SkillsBuddy is a supplement to human coaching, not a replacement. Managers and trainers should use it alongside their own observations.

  

# What happens if the AI gives incorrect feedback?

While the system is tested and monitored, occasional inaccuracies may occur. Use the thumbs up/down feature to provide feedback and discuss results with your coach or manager.

  

# What should I avoid doing with SkillsBuddy?

- Don’t use it for non-Microsoft product scenarios

- Don’t try to game the system for high scores

- Don’t rely solely on AI feedback for performance evaluation
