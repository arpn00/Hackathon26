# ZebraAI Dataflow
ZebraAI pulls data from a variety of source systems in a number of different ways. The diagram below shows at a high level how data gets into ZebraAI

![Dataflow](/wiki/imgs/ZebraAI-Dataflow.png)

Commercial Case Data, for example, is sourced from UDP as well as from Cornerstone.  While the bulk of the data needed comes from Cornerstone, Data is first pulled from UDP to establish the filters needed to prevent loading data we cannot have in ZebraAI.  An example of the type of cases we must exclude would be cases involving government agencies.
## Loading process
The loading process depends upon large batch processes that are executed twice daily. The batch processing performed to load ZebraAI involves several key steps to ensure data is ingested, filtered, processed, and made available for use. Data is ingested from multiple sources, including Cornerstone, UDP Data Mart, ICM Cousto Service, MDM System, Bravo System, Sonic Knowledge Management, Value Based Deliverables, and Live Transcript Data, with updates occurring twice a day to keep the system current.
Once ingested, the data undergoes wrangling in Azure Synapse, where it is cleaned, transformed, and prepared for further processing. This includes filtering to ensure compliance with privacy and security policies, particularly for sensitive cases like U.S. Federal or government cases, which are filtered out using UDP data. The data is then staged in Azure Synapse, where initial processing links data from different sources, and Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is scrubbed to ensure privacy compliance. Aggregation of data from various sources creates a comprehensive dataset.
The processed data is integrated into the Zebra system for further processing and preparation for use. It is then pushed to Azure Open AI and Azure AI Search for advanced processing and search capabilities. The data is made available for ingestion into the website, allowing users to access and utilize it on-demand. Additionally, the data is pushed to the reporting layer, where it is available for ingestion into the Microsoft tenant for reporting and analysis.

Future enhancements include plans to move towards near real-time (NRT) data processing, which will reduce latency and improve system efficiency by processing data in smaller, incremental updates rather than large batch transactions. This workflow ensures that ZebraAI is continuously updated with the latest data while maintaining compliance with privacy and security regulations.

##Sources
By source the following sections describe the types of data included in ZebraAI.
### Cornerstone
The data from Cornerstone includes a variety of information essential for processing and managing cases. This data encompasses detailed information about both commercial and consumer cases, with commercial cases being more complex. It includes case notes that provide context and updates on the status of each case, as well as email transcripts of communications related to the cases, which are processed and scrubbed for personally identifiable information (PII). Additionally, Cornerstone provides incident data detailing the nature and resolution of incidents, survey data offering feedback and additional context, and task data outlining the status and updates of tasks associated with the cases. Entitlement data, which helps in managing and resolving cases, is also included. Furthermore, phone call data, including transcripts and details of calls related to the cases, is provided and similarly scrubbed for PII. Lastly, service data, which includes information about the services involved in the cases, is part of the dataset. This comprehensive data is ingested into the system, processed, and used to support various functions and services within ZebraAI.
### UDP
The data pulled from UDP (Unified Data Platform) includes several key elements essential for filtering and processing cases, particularly for compliance and privacy purposes. This data encompasses information about sales units, which helps in identifying and filtering sensitive cases, such as those related to U.S. Federal or government entities. Detailed contract information, not directly available in Cornerstone, is also provided and used to filter out sensitive cases based on contract details. Additionally, UDP supplies primary TPIDs (Tenant Product IDs), which are identifiers associated with customers, used to filter out cases related to specific entities. Service names involved in the cases are included for filtering and compliance purposes. UDP also provides case purge information, indicating whether a case has been purged, such as duplicate cases or those flagged for removal. Time frame data, including whether a case is currently open or has been closed within the last 9 months (6 months for NRT), helps manage data retention policies. Lastly, UDP supplies mapping tables, such as high-level categorical tables like product categories, which are pulled incrementally to ensure they are up-to-date. This comprehensive data enhances the filtering and processing of cases, ensuring sensitive information is handled appropriately and the data remains accurate and current.
### ICM
The data from ICM (Incident and Case Management) includes several key elements essential for managing and processing cases. This data encompasses detailed information about incidents, including the nature, status, and resolution of each incident. It also includes information about individual cases, such as case identifiers, descriptions, and associated metadata. Case notes provide context and a history of actions taken, while email transcripts of communications related to the cases are processed and scrubbed for personally identifiable information (PII). Additionally, ICM provides discussion data from case-related discussions, including chat transcripts and other forms of communication. Event history data details the actions taken and changes made over time for each case. Finally, service data includes information about the services involved in the cases, such as service names and other relevant details. This comprehensive data creates a complete view of each case, ensuring that all relevant information is available for processing and decision-making.
### Sonic
The data from Sonic, a knowledge management system, includes aggregated information from various knowledge management sources across Microsoft. This data encompasses knowledge articles from both internal and external systems, such as learning.microsoft.com and support.microsoft.com. It also includes information from internal wikis, like the CSS wiki and other team-specific wikis, as well as data from training materials, including transcriptions of PowerPoint presentations and training videos provided to new agents. Additionally, Sonic aggregates information from Azure DevOps (ADO) wikis used by various teams and other smaller knowledge management systems and repositories within Microsoft. By consolidating this diverse information into a single feed, Sonic makes it easier to access and manage knowledge across the organization, supporting various functions and services within ZebraAI.
###MDM
The data from the MDM (Master Data Management) system includes comprehensive metadata around cases, which is essential for organizing and managing data effectively. This metadata encompasses information about subscription levels, detailing different subscription tiers and their mappings. It also includes high-level product categories, which help in categorizing and organizing products within the system. Additionally, the MDM system provides detailed contract information, which is crucial for compliance and privacy filtering. This contract data includes identifiers such as Tenant Product IDs (TPIDs) and other contractual details that help in managing and filtering sensitive cases. By providing this metadata, the MDM system ensures that all business-related categorical items are up-to-date and accurately reflected in the data processing workflows, maintaining consistency and accuracy across various data sources and systems.
### Bravo
The data from the Bravo system includes detailed contract information that is essential for compliance and privacy filtering. This data encompasses sales units, which help in identifying and filtering sensitive cases, such as those related to U.S. Federal or government entities. It also includes specific customer information, such as Tenant Product IDs (TPIDs), which are used to filter out cases related to particular entities. Additionally, Bravo provides detailed contract data that is not directly available in Cornerstone, including identifiers and other contractual details. This comprehensive contract information enhances the filtering and processing of cases, ensuring that sensitive information is handled appropriately and that the data remains accurate and up-to-date. By integrating this data, the system can manage and filter cases more effectively, maintaining compliance with privacy and security regulations.
